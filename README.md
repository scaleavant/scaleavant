# ScaleAvant — B2B iGaming Operations & Risk Architecture

**ScaleAvant** (`ScaleAvant Research`, led by **Marcus Vance**) is a B2B operational partner for licensed online casino and sportsbook operators. We architect and operate:

- **Turnkey Casino Launch**: Full PAM, game aggregation, licensing compliance, and operational readiness.
- **24/7 Risk Management & Fraud Desk**: Sub-second multi-accounting graph detection, bonus abuse prevention, and AML escalation pods (`<15m` SLA).
- **Multilingual Player Support**: Native-speaking VIP and Tier-1/Tier-2 player support teams across regulated jurisdictions.
- **Payments & Cashier Optimization**: Smart multi-PSP cascading, 3DS2 friction tuning, and approval rate engineering (`+8–15%` lift).
